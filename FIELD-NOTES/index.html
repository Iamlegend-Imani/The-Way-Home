Field Notes

The work is allowed to evolve.

Field Notes are where unfinished thinking can remain unfinished. They hold questions, contradictions, observations, practices under review, changing interpretations, and ideas that are not yet ready to become curriculum.

Questions are allowed.

A question does not need to become a claim in order to deserve sustained attention.

Contradictions stay visible.

If a source conflicts with itself, the conflict is documented instead of silently repaired.

Corrections are part of the record.

Stronger sourcing or changed understanding should improve the work without pretending the earlier version never existed.

Experience is not proof.

Personal meaning can be honored while remaining distinct from scientific, historical, or universal claims.

The Field Note method

A record of inquiry, not certainty.

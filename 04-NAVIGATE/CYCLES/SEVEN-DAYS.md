# The Seven Days

**Gate IV — Navigate / Cycles**

## Seven days to show up for self

### Source type
**SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION**

This document organizes the supplied notes on the traditional seven-day planetary cycle. Planetary days have a long history in astrology, but the HERU forms, "vault" language, colors, self-belief functions, and psychological interpretations below are preserved as this source teaching's framework rather than presented as scientific fact.

## Core premise

Each week contains seven distinct modes of self-belief. Each day emphasizes a different relationship to self.

**Every force in Nature ultimately feeds one thing: loyalty to your authentic center.**

## What "vault" means in the source

The lecture describes each day as opening a different **vault**: a symbolic archive or storehouse of a category of experience that has occurred "under the Sun."

- Monday: intuition and inner knowing
- Tuesday: action
- Wednesday: thought, analysis, communication
- Thursday: expansion
- Friday: true love
- Saturday: rest, endings, symbolic death/completion
- Sunday: birth, life, ancestry

This is source-teaching imagery, not a scientific claim that planets literally store human events.

## Seven days vs. five active functions

The seven-day cycle is the complete weekly rhythm.

The same notes also describe **five planetary creation tools** between the Moon and the Sun:

**Mars → Mercury → Jupiter → Venus → Saturn**

In this teaching, "active" does not mean constant labor. It means these five are the functional creation sequence after the Moon/inner position and before the Sun/renewed identity. Saturn's function includes structure, restriction, preservation, completion, and rest.

So the architecture is:

**Moon / inner position → five planetary creation functions → Sun / renewed identity**

## The seven functions

### Monday — Moon
**Increase self-trust.**

Inner world, emotion, intuition, solitude. Seek enough quiet to hear the inner position before acting.

### Tuesday — Mars
**Take actions that support yourself.**

Action, will, motivation, assertion. The source connects safe Mars expression with authentic action.

### Wednesday — Mercury
**Speak truthfully to yourself and others.**

Thought, analysis, speech, writing, singing, decision-making. Use communication to make the desired inner form visible.

### Thursday — Jupiter
**Expand your own capacity.**

Growth, future identity, movement from beginner to expanded self. The source names Jupiter "the link between two lands": the land of the current self and the land of the expanded self.

### Friday — Venus
**Practice self-love and healthy love.**

Love, value, harmony, unity, connection, support. Ask whether love is actually supporting life.

### Saturday — Saturn
**Release what no longer serves you and rest.**

Structure, discipline, sustainability, restriction, completion, rest. Preserve enough energy for another cycle to begin.

### Sunday — Sun
**Reconnect with who you are becoming.**

Identity, visible self, birth, life, ancestry. In the weekly sequence, Sunday follows Saturday's completion/rest and begins the next visible cycle.

## One week, seven functions

1. Monday — trust the inner world.
2. Tuesday — move authentically.
3. Wednesday — think and communicate clearly.
4. Thursday — expand capacity.
5. Friday — practice love and support.
6. Saturday — structure, complete, restrict excess, and rest.
7. Sunday — reconnect with identity and what is ready to be born.

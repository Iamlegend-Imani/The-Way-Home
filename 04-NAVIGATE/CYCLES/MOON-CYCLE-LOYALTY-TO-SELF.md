# Moon Cycle: A Journey of Loyalty to Self

**Gate IV — Navigate / Cycles**

## Source type
**SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION**

This document organizes the supplied notes on menstruation, lunar symbolism, ancestry, emotional memory, OSHUN, seasonal full-moon themes, eclipses, and loyalty to self.

The source speaks specifically about women, wombs, menstruation, and ancestry. The Way Home preserves that framing while noting that not all women menstruate or have wombs, and not everyone who menstruates identifies as a woman.

## Important boundary

The notes contain biomedical-sounding claims about the womb, kidneys, bladder, menstrual problems, body shape, fertility, and emotional states. Those claims are **not established medical facts** and should not be used to diagnose symptoms, explain disease, assign blame, or replace healthcare.

The public teaching therefore treats these as **symbolic/metaphysical correspondences inside the source teaching**.

## Core premise of the source

The teaching describes the menstrual cycle as a **journey to the Moon** and uses the Moon as a metaphor for ancestry, intuition, emotional memory, and an inner realm that remains connected to the woman.

A recurring phrase underneath the teaching is:

> **Loyalty to self gives the cycle meaning.**

The source frames the womb as an accountability and manifestation symbol: a place where emotional truth, ancestry, intuition, and creation meet.

## OSHUN in this teaching

The notes associate OSHUN with emotional memory, flow, wellbeing, reproduction, and the storage of emotional history. The source further says the womb should "bow" to emotional wellbeing.

This is a cross-tradition spiritual synthesis in the supplied notes. Historical and theological claims about OSHUN, lunar rulership, and ancestral practice require separate verification before being presented as established tradition.

## Symbolic architecture

The notes connect:

- **Moon** → intuition / ancestral realm / inner reality
- **Earth** → reality
- **OSHUN** → emotional memory / flow
- **Womb** → manifestation pocket / ancestral continuity / accountability to feeling
- **Aries / neck / authenticity** → truth to self
- **Libra / pelvis / balance** → confidence / balance / relational distribution

The teaching's core movement can be summarized as:

**Authenticity should bow to emotional wellbeing.**

In public-facing material, body-shape claims attached to these correspondences should remain explicitly symbolic rather than prescriptive.

## The year as four movements of loyalty

### December · January · February — Renewal season

- **December:** Test loyalty to self.
- **January:** Maintain loyalty to self despite heavy obstacles.
- **February:** Let unsustainable loyalty die.

### March · April · May — Survival into growth

- **March:** Survived loyalty enters spring.
- **April:** Stabilize and grow what survived.
- **May:** The flower of grown loyalty.

### June · July · August — Fertilization, love, harvest

- **June:** Fertilization.
- **July:** Love is born.
- **August:** Harvest the love that was born.

### September · October · November — New loyalties and new movement

- **September:** Equinox / new beginning. Wins from August are distributed into new loyalties.
- **October:** Test new loyalties; develop attraction/hunting skills. The notes associate this period with Draconids and Orionids.
- **November:** Use new loyalty skills to enter new adventures. The notes associate this period with Taurids and Leonids.

Meteor-shower timing and astronomical claims should be verified separately before being labeled Evidence.

## Lunar eclipse in the source

The notes describe:

- Moon = intuition
- Earth = reality
- Lunar eclipse = intuition passing through reality's darkest shadow

The symbolic interpretation is that an eclipse exposes fear, reverses ordinary perception, and can trigger formation of new loyalties to self.

This is an **Exploration** layer. It is not a scientific interpretation of eclipses.

## September equinox in the source

The September equinox is framed as a threshold for forming new loyalties after harvest.

The supplied notes include specific dates and symbolic references to attacks, demons, and meteor showers. Those details should remain source notes until separately researched and contextualized.

## Practice: a monthly loyalty review

Instead of treating the body as a punishment system, The Way Home adapts the usable reflective core of the teaching:

1. What did I feel repeatedly this cycle?
2. Where was I loyal to myself?
3. Where did I override myself?
4. What emotional pattern needs support rather than shame?
5. What needs to end because it cannot be maintained?
6. What wants to grow next?
7. What am I ready to harvest, distribute, or begin again?

## What remains unresolved

The source contains several claims that require either clarification or research before expansion:

- the attribution that "African Originals" call menses "Journey to the Moon"
- the relationship between OSHUN and lunar rulership
- specific womb-angle/body-shape correspondences
- claims that menstrual problems result from emotional or spiritual disloyalty
- Lesotho / Lebanon and "Old Bantu / New Bantu" references
- exact astronomical timing attached to meteor showers and equinox interpretations

These are preserved as open research questions rather than silently converted into fact.

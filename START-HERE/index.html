Know what you are reading

The Way Home separates evidence, inherited or identifiable traditions, and open exploration. Meaning is not automatically evidence, and evidence does not have to erase mystery.

Evidence

Scientific, academic, historical, astronomical, or primary-source material with a basis that can be examined.

Tradition

A teaching rooted in an identifiable spiritual, philosophical, cultural, contemplative, or esoteric lineage.

Exploration

Personal synthesis, symbolic interpretation, hypothesis, lived observation, or an idea still being examined.

Observe before interpreting.
Reflect before believing.

How to study

The point is not accumulation. The point is increasing your ability to observe, discern, integrate, and eventually use the maps without becoming dependent on them.

1. Read for comprehension.
Understand what a teaching is actually saying before deciding whether you agree.
2. Notice the source.
Ask whether you are reading evidence, tradition, source teaching, or exploration.
3. Observe yourself.
Compare the map with lived experience instead of forcing your experience to fit the map.
4. Practice something small.
A concept becomes more useful when it can be observed in ordinary life.
5. Record what changes.
Keep what clarifies. Question what does not. Notice contradictions instead of hiding them.
6. Return.
Revisit the same material later. A living system can reveal something different when the observer has changed.

If you are unsure

Start with Gate I. Ground before Decode. Learn the difference between sensation, emotion, thought, interpretation, intuition, and belief before asking a chart, number, symbol, teacher, or system to define you.

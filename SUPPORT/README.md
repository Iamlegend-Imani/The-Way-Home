# Support The Way Home

## This work is free. Its creation isn't.

The Way Home is being built as an open body of knowledge. Core materials are intended to remain available without a required payment so that a person's ability to access the work is not determined by their ability to pay.

Free access does not mean effortless creation.

Researching, questioning, writing, editing, designing, sourcing, building, maintaining, and revising this work takes time, tools, technology, and sustained attention.

If The Way Home gives you something valuable and you have the capacity to contribute, voluntary support helps create more capacity to keep researching, building, questioning, and expanding the work.

This is Version One. There is much more to build.

> **Take what you need. Support what you can. Help keep The Way Home open.**

## Support model

Support will remain separate from access.

Planned options:
- one-time contribution
- recurring contribution
- choose-your-own amount

No knowledge tier is created by payment. A supporter is helping sustain the work, not purchasing a more spiritually privileged version of it.

Payment links will be added only after the public learning experience and first downloads are ready.

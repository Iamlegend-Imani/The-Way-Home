Reciprocity

This work is free.
Its creation isn't.

The Way Home is intentionally being built as an open body of knowledge. Reading and core resources are not locked behind a required paywall.

Open access is part of the design.

I want the person with plenty and the person with nothing to spare to be able to enter the Library, read the teachings, use the maps, and explore the work.

That does not make the work costless to create. Researching, learning, questioning, writing, editing, understanding, sourcing, designing, building, maintaining, and revising all require time, attention, tools, and resources.

Support is energy exchange.

If something here gives you language, clarity, comfort, curiosity, remembrance, perspective, or a question worth carrying, and you have the capacity to contribute, your support creates more capacity for the work to continue.

No paywall

Core knowledge remains open. Support does not buy a more spiritually valuable version of the work.

No pressure

Take what you need. If contribution is not available to you right now, the door remains open.

Real reciprocity

When contribution is available, it helps fund the next research pass, guide, source review, design, build, and revision.

The exchange

Take what you need.
Support what you can.
Help keep The Way Home open.

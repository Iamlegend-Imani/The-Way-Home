# Gate I — Ground

## Before the universe, meet yourself.

**Question:** What is happening inside me?

Ground develops the capacity to observe body, breath, emotion, thought, and state before assigning spiritual meaning.

### Topics
- embodiment
- breath
- nervous-system awareness
- interoception
- grounding
- meditation and stillness
- emotional awareness
- discernment
- journaling
- equanimity

### Gate principle

> **Observation comes before interpretation.**

Future lessons will distinguish direct experience from the story told about that experience.

Journey to the Moon

The Return to the Inner Realm. A teaching about loyalty to self, ancestry, emotion, inner truth, endings, renewal, and the movement between what is formed in darkness and what becomes visible in the light.

RememberFeelReleaseReturn
Read this as source teaching, not anatomy. The supplied notes include claims about womb shape, kidneys, bladder, menstruation, fertility, body proportions, and health. Those claims are not established medical facts. The Way Home preserves the symbolic framework without using it to diagnose symptoms, explain disease, blame a person for menstrual problems, or replace healthcare.

The premise

Journey to the Moon is the teaching's image for returning inward.

The Moon is used as a metaphor for ancestry, intuition, emotional memory, darkness, and the part of self encountered before anything becomes visible to the world. The source repeatedly returns to one demand: loyalty to self.

The teaching connects this return with menstruation, womb symbolism, ancestry, emotion, seasonal movement, and creation. Those correspondences are preserved as the teaching's internal symbolic language, not as universal biological law.

Twelve small full moons

Twelve months. Twelve returns.

In the latest source notes, the year is also imagined as twelve smaller full moons, twelve opportunities to return to the inner realm, notice what has ripened, and meet a different movement of loyalty to self.

The annual loyalty wheel

Four movements across the year.

The supplied notes arrange the twelve months into four three-month movements: renewal, growth, love and harvest, then new loyalties.

December · January · February

Renewal

December · Test loyalty to self.

Notice what remains true when loyalty is challenged.

January · Maintain loyalty.

Continue where the loyalty remains authentic and sustainable.

February · Death to loyalty that cannot be maintained.

Release what cannot survive the season rather than preserving it from fear.

March · April · May

Growth

March · Survived loyalty enters spring.

What endured becomes available for movement.

April · Growth and stabilization.

Strengthen the loyalty that survived.

May · Flower.

Allow grown loyalty to become visible.

June · July · August

Love & Harvest

June · Fertilization.

What is being seeded or joined?

July · Love is born.

Notice what has become alive enough to be loved and supported.

August · Harvest.

Receive and gather what the season produced.

September · October · November

New Loyalties

September · New beginning.

Use the harvest to form new loyalties and distribute what was gained.

October · Test new loyalties.

The notes connect this month with attraction and hunting imagery. Astronomical references remain a separate verification task.

November · New adventure.

Use the new loyalty skills to move into another terrain.

Exploration · Eclipse

Moon = intuition. Earth = reality.

Within the source teaching, a lunar eclipse symbolizes intuition passing through reality's darkest shadow. It is interpreted as a confrontation with fear and an opportunity to establish new loyalty to self. This is a metaphysical interpretation of an eclipse, not the scientific explanation of the astronomical event.

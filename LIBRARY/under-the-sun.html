Under the Sun

Truth, visibility, the Seven Days of Heru, and the practice of creating yourself into reality. This study edition organizes supplied notes in which the Sun becomes a symbolic teacher of visibility, authenticity, creation, and the movement toward or away from life.

How to read this: the Sun, Moon, Heru identities, “vaults,” north/south movement, and occupational archetypes below belong to the supplied source teaching and are presented as symbolic or metaphysical claims. They are not astronomy, medical science, or universal descriptions of people born under particular signs.
SUN = Truth   ·   SOIL = Reality   ·   SNOW = Lie   ·   WATER = Emotion
Lesson 01

The Sun as giver and taker

The source describes the Sun as both a giver and a taker: what supports life is strengthened, while what cannot remain in truth is exposed, melted, ended, or transformed.

The image is agricultural and alchemical rather than scientific. Sunlight gives life to soil. The same heat melts snow into water. In the teaching, this becomes a way of asking what truth nourishes and what truth dissolves.

Reflection: What becomes more alive when you tell yourself the truth? What cannot survive the same honesty?

Lesson 02

The Sun as visibility

The source calls the Sun “the eyes,” the capacity to see outward from an inner position. Visibility is therefore not just being seen by other people. It is the process through which an inner identity becomes perceptible in reality.

Rather than treating appearance as a fixed object, the teaching asks how emotion, action, communication, expansion, love, rest, and identity alter what becomes visible.

Lesson 03

Seven days, seven faces

The supplied notes describe all seven days, including Moon-day, as the Sun appearing through different dimensions or identities. The Moon is described as an inner influence across the full day, while the Sun is treated as a temporary visible force whose identity changes with the planetary day.

Monday · MoonEmotion and intuition influence what becomes visible.
Tuesday · Mars · Heru the Red OneAction and authenticity influence how you appear in reality.
Wednesday · Mercury · Heru the Ibis/BaboonThought, analysis, diplomacy, and communication shape visibility.
Thursday · Jupiter · Heru the Link Between Two LandsExpansion changes what you can see and what others can see in you.
Friday · Venus · Heru the PhoenixSelf-love, support, harmony, and value shape visible life.
Saturday · Saturn · Heru the Bull of the SkyRest, preservation, endings, and usefulness shape the next cycle.
Sunday · Sun · HeruIdentity, birth, life, ancestry, and renewed visibility.
Lesson 04

Muumbi: creating yourself into reality

The source says the Seven Days help a person show up as “Muumbi” in order to create the self and become useful to Earth, understood here as reality.

The practical movement is gradual: develop skills, strengthen capacities, become more able to create, exchange, and collaborate, and use repetition to become more capable today than yesterday.

Within the source, movement “north” symbolizes movement toward life; movement “south” symbolizes movement toward death. The Way Home treats these as symbolic directional metaphors, not judgments about geography or people.

Lesson 05

Multidimensionality

To become one-dimensional, in the source teaching, is to move in only one direction or master only one path. To become multidimensional is to develop multiple capacities and learn to combine them.

This is less about collecting titles than integration. The question is not “How many things can I do?” but “What becomes possible when several distinct capacities can work together inside one person?”

Practice: name three skills you already possess. What can exist only because those three skills can meet?

Lesson 06

The nude state: authenticity before performance

The source uses nudity as a metaphor for what cannot be copied: the irreducible quality of being oneself before imitation, branding, role, or performance. Some raw notes extend this into a sign-by-sign map of “people in their nude state.”

Aries / Auset
Creators, pioneers, inventors, founders.
Taurus / Ausar
Channels, supply and delivery systems, entrepreneurs, diplomats, singers.
Gemini / Anubis
Thinkers, therapists, psychics, diviners, nail technicians.
Cancer / Ptah
Feelers, seers, healthcare practitioners, traditional herbalists.
Leo / Amun
Lovers, fashion enthusiasts, media personalities, public figures, stylists.
Virgo / Ra
Analysts, self-defense teachers, wellness protectors, boundary inventors, investigators.
Libra / Sobek
Balancers, police, justice-system roles, prosecutors, auctioneers.
Scorpio / Hathor
Attraction-oriented roles including midwifery, sex work, real estate, ushering, celebrity, advertising, marketing, sales, and trading.
Sagittarius / Khnum
Adventurers, pattern recognizers, astrologers, astronomers, journalists, gossipers.
Aquarius / Set
Intellectuals, educators, writers, history and record keepers.

Incomplete source: this note set does not include Capricorn or Pisces in the “nude state” vocation list. They are not invented here. Historical grounding for the deity/sign pairings remains a separate research task.

Use the teaching without becoming trapped by it.

  • What truth is giving life to something in me?
  • What truth is dissolving something I can no longer maintain?
  • Which daily function do I overuse?
  • Which function would make me more multidimensional?
  • What part of me remains recognizably mine even when others imitate the surface?
  • What becomes more useful when I become more authentic?

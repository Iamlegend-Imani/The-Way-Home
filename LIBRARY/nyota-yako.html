NYOTA YAKO
Your Star Quality

Understanding the Solar System You Were Born Into. An eight-lesson study module built from supplied notes on birth charts, planetary functions, zodiac archetypes, higher octaves, collective planetary weather, and the practice of reading one's own star quality.

How The Way Home handles this material: this module preserves the source teaching's terminology and internal logic. Astrology, planetary correspondences, body symbolism, quantum-jump language, and archetypal claims are presented as source teaching and exploration, not as established scientific or medical fact. Unfinished and contradictory notes stay visible rather than being silently repaired.
Lesson 01

The Solar System You Were Born Into

NYOTA YAKO begins with the arrangement of the Solar System at birth, described in the notes as what the modern world calls a birth chart.

  • Sun: how the world sees you; memory; public function.
  • Moon: who you are alone; feelings; self-function.
  • Mars: behavior and action.
  • Mercury: thought, communication, and self-analysis.
  • Venus: love, support, balance, and value.
  • Jupiter: expansion and what one rises into.
  • Saturn: rest, usefulness, and restriction.
  • Uranus, Neptune, Pluto: higher-octave and transformation language.

Practice: record placements before interpreting them, then separate placement, teaching, and lived observation.

Recovered reading method: planet = what is speaking; sign = how it speaks; house = where it speaks; aspects = how the voices interact. Open the chart-reading scaffold →
Lesson 02

Sun, Moon & Rising: Three Ways You Exist

Sun

How the world sees you and what you are born to do for the world.

Moon

Who you are to self when nobody is watching.

Jupiter / Rising

How you see yourself and what you are born to rise into and become.

The source combines Jupiter and Rising language in a way that differs from standard astrology. Version 1 preserves that wording rather than pretending the distinction does not exist.

Source principle: Do not be shamed out of your essence.

Lesson 03

The Planetary Functions

  • Mercury: communication, thought, self-analysis.
  • Venus: how to love; support, value, balance.
  • Mars: how you behave and take action.
  • Jupiter: expansion, growth, level-up.
  • Saturn: how you rest; usefulness and restriction.
  • Uranus: “Quantum Jump”; higher octave of Mercury.
  • Neptune: “Look of Final Avatar”; higher octave of Venus.
  • Pluto: transformation into a new creature; higher-octave transformation.
Open question: the notes call this section “7 Pillars” but currently list eight planetary functions. Version 1 does not decide which one to remove.
Lesson 04

Where the Planets Are Safe

The latest notes deepen “safe in” from a placement list into a teaching about the conditions under which each function can be used well.

Sun · Leo

Heartspace. Engage identity and visibility with love.

Moon · Cancer

Intuition and feelings. Feelings are used to teach patience.

Mars · Aries

Action is safe when authenticity is present. Authenticity is linked with kindness.

Mercury · Gemini

Communication is safe when self-thinking is active. Self-thinking is linked with honesty.

Jupiter · Sagittarius

Expansion is safe in adventure and change, with protection of self.

Venus · Taurus

Love is safe in support and wealth. Nurture love through support, wellness, health, and value.

Saturn · Capricorn

Usefulness and rest. Preserve energy for future time, expansion, and hope.

The Way Home preserves safe in as source language. Later historical research can compare it with rulership, domicile, exaltation, and other astrological traditions without pretending they are identical.

Lesson 06

Higher Octaves & Transformation

The source describes Mercury → Uranus, Venus → Neptune, and Mars/Jupiter/Saturn → Pluto through higher-octave language. It also distinguishes lower-octave transformation, such as poverty to billionaire, from higher-octave transformation described as becoming a new creature or species.

Language boundary: “Quantum Jump” is preserved here as metaphysical language. It is not presented as a claim about quantum mechanics.

New source queue: the latest notes add orbital-cycle numbers and “graduation” language to this section. Those numbers are being preserved in the private archive and verified before they are presented publicly as astronomy.
Lesson 07

Where Is the Solar System Today?

The notes apply the same planetary functions to current placements to ask where the collective is now. Because placements change, this becomes a dated Field Note method rather than a permanent interpretation page.

Each entry should record date, chart source, placements, source interpretation, and direct observation. The key question is: What is happening, what does the framework suggest, and what do I actually see?

Lesson 08

Read Your Own Star Quality

Pull your chart, learn each planetary function before interpreting the sign, read Sun/Moon/Rising as three identity positions, compare every interpretation with lived reality, and look for repeating qualities across placements.

End every reading with four questions:

  • What does this map help me notice?
  • What requires further observation?
  • What do I reject or leave open?
  • What will I actually practice?

Gate principle: A map should help you encounter yourself, not replace yourself.

The Seven Days

A recovered weekly rhythm from The Intuitive System: seven planetary days used as a symbolic self-support cycle of vision, intuition, action, communication, expansion, love, and rest.

Source boundary: this page preserves the correspondences explicitly present in the supplied teaching guide. It is a symbolic/metaphysical framework, not a scientific claim. Where the source did not state a color, this guide says so instead of inventing one.
01

Vault: Vision, birth, life, ancestry. Practice: see and support the self; hold the head and truth upright.

Yellow

Vault: Expansion; the link between two lands. Practice: bridge the beginner self and the more expanded self.

Pink / Green
07

Saturday · Saturn

Vault: Rest, death, restriction, karma. Practice: rest, complete, restrict excess, and allow an old version to end.

Black

Try the rhythm

One week. Seven functions.

  1. Give each day one small visible act that matches its function.
  2. Record the inner feeling before the act and the visible result after it.
  3. On Saturday, include actual rest or completion.
  4. On Sunday, name what feels ready to be born after that rest.
  5. At the end of the week, ask which function you naturally use and which you avoid.

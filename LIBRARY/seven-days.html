The Seven Days

Seven days to show up for self. In this source teaching, the traditional seven-day planetary cycle becomes seven distinct modes of self-belief: inner trust, action, thought, expansion, love, structure/rest, and renewed identity.

Source boundary: this page organizes the supplied teaching notes. Planetary days have a long history in astrology, but the HERU forms, “vault” language, psychological meanings, color correspondences, and self-belief framework below are presented as this source teaching's symbolic system rather than scientific fact.
Seven days vs. five active functions: the seven-day cycle is the complete weekly rhythm. The same notes also describe five planetary creation tools after the Moon and before the Sun: Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, and Saturn. In that five-function sequence, “active” means a functional part of creation, not constant exertion. Saturn's function is specifically structure, preservation, completion, and rest. Moon holds the inner position; Sun represents identity/birth and renewed visibility.
What does “vault” mean here? The lecture describes each day as opening a different symbolic archive of what has happened “under the Sun.” Monday is the vault of intuition; Tuesday action; Wednesday thought and communication; Thursday expansion; Friday true love; Saturday rest/death/completion; Sunday birth/life. The “vault” is therefore a metaphorical storehouse or category of experience inside this teaching.
M

Monday · Moon · HERU MOTHER / AUSET

Increase self-trust.

Aspect of self: inner world, emotion, intuition, who you are when no one is watching.

Practice: seek silence and aloneness; listen before acting; ask what your feelings and intuition are actually saying.

Vault of intuition: within the source teaching, Monday symbolically holds the record of intuitive processes and inner knowing.

Tuesday · Mars · HERU THE RED ONE

Take actions that support yourself.

Aspect of self: action, will, motivation, assertion, authentic motion.

Practice: address the block, choose the action, and move in a way that remains truthful to self.

Vault of action: the source describes Tuesday as the symbolic archive of actions taken under the Sun.

Red
W

Wednesday · Mercury · HERU THE IBIS & BABOON

Speak truthfully to yourself and others.

Aspect of self: thought, speech, analysis, communication, decision-making.

Practice: clarify the question; speak, write, sing, analyze, or communicate in the form you actually want to embody.

Vault of thought and communication: Wednesday symbolically gathers thinking, analysis, speech, and communication.

Th

Thursday · Jupiter · HERU LINK BETWEEN TWO LANDS

Expand your own capacity.

Aspect of self: growth, future identity, possibility, movement from beginner to expanded self.

Practice: ask what the next land looks like, what capacity it requires, and what bridge can be built today.

Vault of expansion: Thursday is framed as the archive of expansion, linking the land of who you are now with the land of who you can become.

Earth / Skin Tones
F

Friday · Venus · HERU THE PHOENIX

Practice self-love and healthy love.

Aspect of self: love, value, harmony, unity, support, connection.

Practice: restore peace where possible; address wounds around worth and connection; ask whether love is actually supporting life.

Vault of love: in the lecture, Friday is the symbolic archive of “true love” that has occurred under the Sun. It is not a literal cosmic database claim; it is the source teaching's image for accessing the function of love.

Pink
Sa

Saturday · Saturn · HERU BULL OF THE SKY

Release what no longer serves you and rest.

Aspect of self: structure, discipline, preservation, restriction, completion, sustainable commitment.

Practice: organize energy and time; finish what needs finishing; restrict waste; preserve capacity; rest enough for another cycle to begin.

Vault of rest, endings, and completion: the source groups rest and symbolic “death” here as the ending of a version or cycle rather than only literal death.

Black
Su

Sunday · Sun · HERU

Reconnect with who you are becoming.

Aspect of self: identity, visible self, ego/personality, birth, renewed life.

Practice: return to the center; examine self-image; name what is ready to be born after Saturday's completion and rest.

Vault of birth and life: the source places Sunday after Saturnian rest and describes it as the archive of birth, life, and ancestry.

Try one complete week

Seven days to show up for self.

  • MondayIncrease self-trust. Give your inner world enough quiet to become audible.
  • TuesdayTake one authentic action that directly supports you.
  • WednesdaySay, write, or communicate one thing more truthfully.
  • ThursdayExpand one capacity: learn, travel, ask, imagine, practice, or bridge toward the future self.
  • FridayPractice love that includes support, worth, harmony, and healthy connection.
  • SaturdayStructure, restrict excess, complete, preserve energy, and actually rest.
  • SundayReconnect with identity. Ask what is ready to be born into the next week.

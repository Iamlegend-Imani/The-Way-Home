# Start Here

The Way Home is designed as a sequence, not a pile of spiritual concepts.

## Recommended path

**Ground → Know → Decode → Navigate → Create → Remember → Expand → Return**

If you are new to spirituality, metaphysics, astrology, symbolic systems, or consciousness study, begin with **Ground** and move in order.

## Three lenses

Every major claim should be identified as one of the following:

- **EVIDENCE** — established evidence or well-sourced history
- **TRADITION** — a teaching rooted in an identifiable tradition
- **EXPLORATION** — personal synthesis, symbolic interpretation, hypothesis, or open inquiry

## First principle

> **Observe before interpreting. Reflect before believing.**

The Way Home is meant to increase discernment and self-knowledge, not dependence on a system, teacher, chart, number, or external authority.

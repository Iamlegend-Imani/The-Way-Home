# Master Knowledge Inventory

This file is the working archaeology of **The Way Home**. It gathers concepts, practices, frameworks, questions, and artifacts developed or explored over time so they can be organized without losing their history.

The inventory is intentionally broader than the final curriculum. Inclusion here does not mean endorsement, proof, or publication-ready status.

## Inventory Rules

Every item should eventually be tagged with:

- **Origin** — personal framework, named tradition, sourced teaching, research finding, or open question
- **Knowledge lens** — EVIDENCE, TRADITION, or EXPLORATION
- **Gate** — where it belongs in the learning sequence
- **Status** — raw note, needs sourcing, needs revision, ready to teach, or ready to publish
- **Artifact** — lesson, essay, worksheet, diagram, field note, glossary entry, or download

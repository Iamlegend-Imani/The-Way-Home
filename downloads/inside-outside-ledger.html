Inside-Outside Ledger

A practice for comparing inner knowing with visible support.

Choose one area of life where there may be a gap between what you know privately and what you support publicly. Record the inner truth, what you say, what you repeatedly do, and the smallest visible action that would bring the two closer together.

What I know alone What I say in light What I repeatedly do Next act of support
Reflection: Where do your words and repeated actions already match what you know? Where is the smallest honest adjustment available now?

Let inner truth and visible action become one movement.

The Eight Gates

The sequence is not a hierarchy of spiritual worth. It is a learning path: begin with direct experience, move through self-knowledge, maps, cycles, creation, ancestry and expanded inquiry, then return to a life you can actually inhabit.

Exploration · Why Eight?

∞

Return is part of the loop.

Eight began as instructional architecture. It also became a symbol: turned sideways, the number evokes infinity. The Way Home uses that visual relationship as exploration, not as a historical claim about the infinity sign.

Gate VIII is not an ending. Return curves back toward Ground at another level of awareness. The cycle repeats because the person moving through it is no longer exactly the same.

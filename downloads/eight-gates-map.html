The Eight Gates

A Human Operating System for Conscious Living.

The Way Home is sequenced so that observation and self-knowledge come before increasingly symbolic and metaphysical interpretation. Move through the Gates in order if you are new to the material.

GROUND → KNOW → DECODE → NAVIGATE → CREATE → REMEMBER → EXPAND → RETURN
01 · Ground

Meet yourself first.

What is happening inside me?

02 · Know

Study the inner landscape.

Who am I beneath conditioning?

03 · Decode

Learn the maps.

What maps help me understand myself?

04 · Navigate

Work with time and place.

How do cycles, relationships, and place affect me?

05 · Create

Participate consciously.

How do I consciously participate in my life?

06 · Remember

Study what came before.

What came before me and what do I carry forward?

07 · Expand

Enter advanced inquiry.

What might consciousness, spirit, and reality be?

08 · Return

Live what you learn.

How do I live what I have learned?

Method: EVIDENCE identifies established or well-sourced material. TRADITION identifies teachings with an identifiable lineage. EXPLORATION identifies synthesis, symbolic interpretation, hypothesis, or open inquiry.

Observe before interpreting. Reflect before believing.

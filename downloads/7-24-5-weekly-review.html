7–24–5 Weekly Review

Three overlapping layers from The Intuitive System.

This page keeps three different symbolic layers separate. Seven is the complete weekly self-support cycle. Twenty-four is the full-day inner atmosphere of feeling and self-love. Five refers to the five planetary creation functions between Moon and Sun in the supplied notes: Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, and Saturn. “Active” here means functional in the creation sequence; Saturn's function includes completion, preservation, restriction, and rest.

Architecture:
Moon / inner position → Mars → Mercury → Jupiter → Venus → Saturn → Sun / renewed identity

Rhythm: Seven days to support self. Twenty-four hours to tend the inner atmosphere. Five creation functions to move, communicate, expand, love/support, structure, complete, and rest.

7–24–5 Weekly Review

A reflection tool from The Intuitive System.

This worksheet preserves the symbolic 7–24–5 rhythm used in The Intuitive System: seven-day support, twenty-four-hour self-love and inner atmosphere, and five-day creation and exchange. Use it as a reflective framework, not as a biological or medical claim.

Rhythm: Seven days to support yourself. Twenty-four hours to love and feel yourself. Five active days to interact, create, and exchange.
Cycle What I practiced What entered What became visible What changes next
7-day support
24-hour self-love
5-day creation
End-of-week questions:
What entered? What moved? What did I support? What became visible? What is ready to be exchanged?

Support. Feel. Create. Review. Adjust.

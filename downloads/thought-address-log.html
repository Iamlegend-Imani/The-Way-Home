Thought-Address Log

A discernment practice from The Intuitive System.

Use this log to slow down the moment a strong thought arrives. The original teaching asks the learner to notice the thought, consider its felt source, observe the body, notice its effect on self-love, and decide whether to keep, release, or let it pass through.

Practice sequence: Listen → Locate → Choose → Receive → Record.
Thought Memory / intuition / collective / passage Body signal Self-love effect Keep / release Possible reality

The arrival of a thought does not require you to make it yours.

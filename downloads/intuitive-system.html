The Intuitive System

The Body as a Map of Thought, Intuition, and Reality. A 12-lesson framework exploring memory, intuition, self-support, self-love, symbolic anatomy, ancestry, planetary forces, discernment, and manifestation.

Source TeachingExplorationNot Clinical Guidance
How to read this material. The original guide was structured from a supplied lecture transcript. It preserves a spiritual and metaphysical correspondence system in which anatomy, astrology, ancestry, time, thought, and self-love mirror one another. The Way Home presents those correspondences as the source teaching's framework, not as established medical or biological fact.

The system in one movement

Collect thought → Feel it → Love it → Digest it → Support it → Make it visible

The guide organizes the lecture as a six-stage creation cycle. Memory and intuition enter the system, move through feeling, self-love, and analysis, and are then either supported or withheld in visible life.

Three symbolic rhythms

7 days

Seven cervical vertebrae are used symbolically to represent voluntary self-support, truth, and visible action across a weekly planetary cycle.

24 hours

The thoracic region and ribs are used symbolically to represent the full-day inner atmosphere of self-feeling, self-love, information, and access.

5 active days

Five lumbar vertebrae are used symbolically to represent interaction, ingestion, analysis, work, creation, and exchange.

One operating system

The framework combines support, inner atmosphere, and active creation into a recurring practice of observation and alignment.

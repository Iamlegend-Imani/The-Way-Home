The Intuitive System

The Body as a Map of Thought, Intuition, and Reality. This public study edition introduces the 12-lesson framework, its practices, and its symbolic logic while making its boundaries explicit.

Tradition / Source TeachingExplorationNot Clinical Guidance
How to read this material.

The original teaching guide was structured from a supplied lecture transcript. It preserves a spiritual and metaphysical correspondence system in which anatomy, astrology, ancestry, time, thought, and self-love are read as mirrors of one another. The Way Home presents those correspondences as part of the source teaching, not as established medical or biological fact. Do not use this material to diagnose or treat a health condition.

The system in one movement

Collect thought → Feel it → Love it → Digest it → Support it → Make it visible

The guide organizes the lecture as a six-stage creation cycle. Memory and intuition enter the system, move through feeling, self-love, and analysis, and are then either supported or withheld in visible life.

Three symbolic rhythms

7 days

Seven cervical vertebrae are used symbolically to represent voluntary self-support, truth, and visible action across a weekly planetary cycle.

24 hours

The thoracic region and ribs are used symbolically to represent the full-day inner atmosphere of self-feeling, self-love, information, and access.

5 active days

Five lumbar vertebrae are used symbolically to represent interaction, ingestion, analysis, work, creation, and exchange.

One operating system

The framework combines support, inner atmosphere, and active creation into a recurring practice of observation and alignment.

The 12 lessons

Lesson 01

The Body Is a Language

Introduces the source's method of reading placement, visibility, enclosure, number, and physical function as symbolic relationships.

Lesson 02

Gemini and the Two Attachments

Uses the arms as a symbolic model for self-thinking, visible self-support, and an involuntary connection to the inner realm.

Lesson 03

The Right Arm: Memory

Frames the right arm as memory-based, learned, visible thought and asks how history shapes chosen movement.

Lesson 04

The Left Arm: Intuition

Frames the left arm as intuitive and body-carried thought, with self-love and self-kindness as organizing principles.

Lesson 05

The Great Trinity

Maps Cancer, Leo, and Virgo to feeling, love/transport, and analysis/digestion inside the source's inner creation chamber.

Lesson 06

The Spine as Creation Pillar

Introduces the 7–12–5 symbolic spine map and the distinction between inner processes and outward support.

Lesson 07

Seven Cervical Portals

Connects seven symbolic cervical portals with the planetary week: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, and Saturn.

Lesson 08

The Thoracic Clock

Examines the twenty-four-hour inner atmosphere through ancestry, information, relationships, access, and self-love.

Lesson 09

The Lumbar Five-Day Cycle

Expands "ingestion" beyond literal food to include people, information, work, places, and repeated interaction.

Lesson 10

Inner Will, Outer Support

Examines the gap between what a person recognizes privately and what they visibly say, do, and support.

Lesson 11

The Womb Blueprint

Uses womb and arm imagery as a symbolic model for receiving potential, discerning thought, and choosing what to carry forward.

Lesson 12

The Self-Love Operating System

Integrates the 7–24–5 framework around self-support, self-love, refusal, creation, usefulness, and exchange.

The Way Home treats these as symbolic extensions contained in the source teaching.

# Lesson 01 — The Solar System You Were Born Into

**Label:** SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION

## What the teaching says

NYOTA YAKO / YOUR STAR QUALITY begins with the arrangement of the Solar System at birth. In the notes, that arrangement is described as what the modern world calls a **birth chart**.

The teaching states that a person cannot fully know who they are without understanding the Solar System they live in and the planetary arrangement present when they were born.

## Core planetary orientation

Within the source notes:

- **Sun** — how the world sees you; memory; what you are born to do for the world.
- **Moon** — who you are when alone; feelings; what you are born to do for self.
- **Mars** — behavior and action; linked in the notes to actions ancestors have taken.
- **Mercury** — thought, communication, thinking for self, and self-analysis.
- **Venus** — love, support, balance, value, and relationship.
- **Jupiter** — expansion, growth, level-up, and what one rises into.
- **Saturn** — rest, usefulness, restriction, and responsibility.
- **Uranus** — quantum-jump language; described as a higher octave of Mercury.
- **Neptune** — the look of the "final avatar"; described as a higher octave of Venus.
- **Pluto** — transformation into a new creature; described as a higher octave involving Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn.

## What this lesson does not establish

This module does not present planetary positions as scientifically proven causes of personality, anatomy, behavior, or destiny. It presents the source teaching as a symbolic interpretive system.

## Practice

Pull your birth chart from a chart calculator and write the sign placement of each planet without interpreting anything yet.

Then separate three columns:

1. **Placement** — what the chart says.
2. **Teaching** — what NYOTA YAKO says that placement represents.
3. **Observation** — what you can actually recognize in your life.

## Reflection

- What did you notice before interpreting?
- Which placements feel immediately familiar?
- Which do not?
- Can you hold a symbolic map without forcing your life to fit it?

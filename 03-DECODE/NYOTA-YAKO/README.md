# NYOTA YAKO / YOUR STAR QUALITY

## Understanding the Solar System You Were Born Into

**Gate:** III — Decode  
**Status:** Version 1 teaching module  
**Primary label:** SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION

NYOTA YAKO begins with a central proposition from the supplied notes: the arrangement of the Solar System at birth is a map of self, commonly called a birth chart in modern astrology.

The Way Home preserves the language and internal logic of this teaching while separating it from established scientific claims. Astrology and the symbolic correspondences in this module are presented as a source teaching and interpretive framework, not as proven causal biology or physics.

## Module question

**What can the sky at birth help me notice about myself without allowing the map to replace direct self-knowledge?**

## Lessons

1. **The Solar System You Were Born Into** — birth chart, Sun, Moon, planets, and the basic Nyota Yako premise.
2. **Sun, Moon & Rising: Three Ways You Exist** — self to world, self in private, and the self one rises into.
3. **The Planetary Functions** — Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto within the source framework.
4. **Where the Planets Are Safe** — the source teaching's "safe in" correspondences and the open questions that remain.
5. **The Twelve Zodiac Archetypes** — zodiac signs paired with named archetypes, body correspondences, elements, and public functions.
6. **Higher Octaves & Transformation** — Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, quantum-jump language, final-avatar language, and transformation.
7. **Where Is the Solar System Today?** — using current placements as a dated collective-weather practice.
8. **Read Your Own Star Quality** — a practical interpretation process that ends in observation rather than blind belief.

## Open questions preserved from the notes

- The notes call one section **"7 Pillars"** but currently list eight planetary functions. Version 1 does not silently resolve that contradiction.
- Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto are left blank in the source's **"safe in"** list.
- Some archetype names and spellings should be researched before being presented as historical Egyptian/Kemetic claims.
- Body and health correspondences are preserved as symbolic claims within the source teaching and are not medical predictions.

# Lesson 05 — The Twelve Zodiac Archetypes

**Label:** SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION  
**Safety note:** Body and health statements below are preserved as symbolic claims from the source notes. They are not medical predictions, diagnoses, or evidence that a sign determines anatomy or health.

The notes pair each zodiac sign with a named archetype and describe a self-quality, element, body correspondence, behavioral pattern, and public function.

## Aries / AUSET
- Born truthful to self and in a truthful relationship with self.
- Associated in the notes with clairvoyance, telepathy, and psychometry.
- Body correspondence: head and nervous system.
- Public function: teach the world how to plant new traits about self by listening to the body and acting in alignment.
- Element instruction: interactive because Fire is described as alchemist.
- Source principle: authenticity flows; inauthenticity creates awkwardness.

## Taurus / AUSAR
- Born with high self-worth and self-value.
- Naturally self-supportive and vocal about self-principles.
- Body correspondence: voice, neck, and bones.
- Public function: teach the world how to embody the seven-day planets and show up daily for self.
- Associated with abundance as the visible result of self-support.
- Element instruction: realistic because Earth is described as reality.

## Gemini / ANUBIS — Masters of HEKA
- Born an independent thinker.
- Special thinking, communication, and decision-making skills.
- Body correspondence: arms and muscles.
- Public function: generate, recall, and store information; embody independence.
- The notes associate Gemini with channels, tarot readers, psychics, and mediums.
- Element instruction: "gossip active" because Air is linked to information movement.

## Cancer / PTAH
- Born in an intimate relationship with solace.
- Described as present with the Infinite through feeling.
- Body correspondence: chest, breasts, and lungs.
- Public function: teach abundance, love, intuition, and how to "see in the dark" within self.
- Emotions are described metaphorically as rain that can help others grow.
- Element instruction: emotional and loyal to emotion because Water is described as creator.

## Leo / AMUN
- Born self-loving and self-expressing.
- Born in deep relationship with Creation.
- Body correspondence: heart, blood, and circulatory system.
- Public function: teach self-expression, self-love, and love of all that is created; Ubuntu-oriented.
- Element instruction: interactive because Fire is described as alchemist.

## Virgo / RA
- Born analyst and connected to all.
- Body correspondence: stomach and digestive system.
- Public function: serve and protect self by "doing no evil and accepting no evil."
- Associated with five daily transitions connected in the notes to Mars, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, and Saturn after removing Sun and Moon.
- Element instruction: realistic because Earth is described as reality.

## Libra / SOBEK
- Born balanced.
- The notes use the body divided by the buttocks/"nyash" as a symbolic image of balance.
- Body correspondence: urinary system; the notes also make a body-shape claim that should remain symbolic rather than medical.
- Public function: truthful judgment, command of the outside environment, and management of the birth, storage, and distribution of energies harvested from "infinite waters."
- The notes call Libra Air in one place but also contain a line saying "because she is Water"; this contradiction is preserved for source review.

## Scorpio / HATHOR
- Born attractive.
- Associated with attracting valuable mates.
- Body correspondence: private/sexual organs.
- Public function: teach embodiment of sexuality and visible essence.
- Element instruction: emotionally active because Water.

## Sagittarius / KHUM
- Born self-adventurous.
- Born in a strong relationship with wilderness and exploration.
- Body correspondence: skin and nails.
- Public function: link the public between two lands, described as past and future.
- Element instruction: interactive because Fire is described as alchemist.

## Capricorn / THOT — Endocrine System
- Born self-useful and able to start from scratch.
- Described as "darlings of the Collective Consciousness."
- Publicly perceived positively within the source framework.
- Consistency is described as a superpower through showing up.
- Body correspondence: knees; the heading also associates Capricorn with the endocrine system.
- Public function: teach the public how to be useful to self today to avoid misuse and fatigue tomorrow.
- Element instruction: realistic because Earth is described as reality.

## Aquarius / SET
- Born self-knowing.
- Born able to read and impact Creation.
- Body correspondence: Achilles heels.
- Described as highly intellectual and quick.
- Public function: embody one's obligation in the "great circle of life" quickly.
- Element instruction: "gossip active" because Air.

## Pisces / HERU
- Born self-believing.
- Born able to sacrifice self in service of those in need.
- Body correspondence: feet and immune system.
- Element instruction: emotionally active because Water.

## How to study this lesson

Do not treat the list as a biological checklist. Study it as an internal symbolic system:

1. Identify the sign's stated **self-quality**.
2. Identify its **public function**.
3. Identify the **element instruction**.
4. Note the **body symbol** without turning it into a medical prediction.
5. Ask what is source teaching, what might have a historical lineage, and what remains exploration.

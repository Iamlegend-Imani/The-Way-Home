# Lesson 04 — Where the Planets Are Safe

**Label:** SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION

The supplied notes use the phrase **"safe in"** for certain planet-sign relationships.

## Source correspondences

- **Sun is safe in Leo**
- **Moon is safe in Cancer**
- **Mars is safe in Aries** — behavior is described as being meant to be authentic.
- **Mercury is safe in Gemini and Virgo** — thinking for self and self-analysis.
- **Venus is safe in Taurus and Libra** — love is described as safe in wealth, support, and balance.
- **Jupiter is safe in Sagittarius** — linked in the notes to travel and expansion.
- **Saturn is safe in Capricorn** — rest linked to usefulness to self.
- **Uranus is safe in** — unfinished in source notes.
- **Neptune is safe in** — unfinished in source notes.
- **Pluto is safe in** — unfinished in source notes.

## Why the wording matters

The Way Home preserves **safe in** because that is the language of the source teaching. Later research can compare this with concepts such as rulership or domicile in other astrological traditions, but those comparisons should be added as research rather than silently substituted.

## Practice

Look at your chart and note whether any planets occupy a sign listed as "safe" in this framework.

Do not conclude that an unsafely placed planet is defective. Instead ask:

- What quality does this framework associate with the planet?
- What quality does it associate with its "safe" sign?
- What tension or adaptation do I actually observe in life?

## Open research questions

- What is the source lineage for the term "safe"?
- Were Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto intentionally left open because the teaching was unfinished?
- How does this framework relate to classical, Hellenistic, modern, or other astrological rulership systems?

# Lesson 02 — Sun, Moon & Rising: Three Ways You Exist

**Label:** SOURCE TEACHING / EXPLORATION

## The three positions in the notes

The supplied notes give three especially important functions:

- **Sun sign** — how the world sees you; what you are born to do for the world.
- **Moon sign** — who you are to yourself; how you are when alone and nobody is watching.
- **Jupiter / Rising** — how you see yourself and what you were born to rise into and become.

The source uses **Jupiter/Rising** together in a way that is not standard astrological terminology. Version 1 preserves that wording rather than silently correcting it.

## A three-part reading

### Sun — public essence
Ask:
- What quality does the teaching associate with my Sun sign?
- How does that quality show up when I am visible to others?
- Where do I resist being seen that way?

### Moon — private essence
Ask:
- What am I like when no one is watching?
- What emotional pattern appears when social performance falls away?
- What do I consistently need from myself?

### Rising / Jupiter — expansion identity
Ask:
- What kind of self am I repeatedly growing toward?
- What conditions make me feel more expansive?
- What does "rising into" this placement mean in lived behavior rather than fantasy?

## Source principle

> **Do not be shamed out of your essence.**

The notes repeatedly frame authenticity as necessary to living one's role fully.

## Reflection before believing

A chart interpretation is most useful when it produces a question you can test in your life. It becomes less useful when it becomes an excuse, identity prison, or claim that contradicts direct experience.

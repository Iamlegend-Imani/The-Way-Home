# Sources

The Sources section exists so readers can distinguish where ideas come from and verify what can be verified.

## Source-note goals

For each major topic, document:
- origin and historical context
- primary or authoritative sources where available
- major traditions or schools of interpretation
- what is established evidence
- what is contested
- what is metaphysical or symbolic
- what is original synthesis within The Way Home

## Priority research areas
- astrology history and traditions
- numerology traditions
- Human Design origins
- astrocartography origins and method
- Jungian archetypes and synchronicity
- meditation and contemplative traditions
- karma, samsara, dharma, equanimity, and samadhi
- chakra and kundalini traditions
- sacred geometry traditions
- epigenetics and intergenerational inheritance
- modern manifestation traditions
- Focus Wheel / Abraham-Hicks lineage
- quantum mechanics versus spiritual quantum language

No source list should be used to manufacture certainty where the evidence does not support it.
